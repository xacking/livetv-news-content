Privacy Policy

Ontivo: News, Radio & Live TV (Android app ID com.livetv.news). Last updated: 17 October 2026.

Ontivo has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.

1. What stays on your phone

Your edition (country), interests, saved stories, reading/listening streak, XP, quiz results and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

2. Content you see

Headlines come from Google News RSS and link to the original publishers; opening a story takes you to the publisher's website, which has its own privacy policy. Radio stations come from the public radio-browser.info directory and stream directly from each station. TV streams, when available in your country, come from their providers. These services see your IP address when your phone connects to them, as with any website.

3. Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which may also show ads from mediation partners Meta Audience Network and Pangle. These services may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and app-usage information to show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent with Google's consent form before ads are personalised; you can change your choice any time under You → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google → Ads).

  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data from apps
  • Meta Audience Network: facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • Pangle: pangleglobal.com/privacy

4. Analytics, crash reports and notifications (Google Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand which features are used (for example: story opened, quiz completed, radio played, edition), Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports, and Firebase Cloud Messaging to send breaking-news notifications if you allow them. These collect device information, app-instance identifiers and usage events, not your name or contacts. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland analytics only starts after your consent. Notifications can be turned off any time under You → Breaking news alerts or in Android settings.

5. Children

Ontivo is a general-audience news app and is not directed at children under 13.

6. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers above, for the purposes described.

7. Your rights

You can withdraw consent (Privacy choices), reset your advertising ID, turn off notifications, or delete all app data by clearing storage or uninstalling. For any other request, contact us.
